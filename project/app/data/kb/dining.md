# Dining

## Restaurant

**Marea**, on the ground floor, serves breakfast from 06:30 to 10:30, and dinner from 18:30 to 22:30. It is closed for lunch. Reservations are recommended for dinner and can be made at the front desk or by phone.

## Breakfast

Breakfast is a buffet and costs $18 per adult and $9 per child aged 4 to 12. Children under 4 eat free. Some rates include breakfast; whether a given reservation includes it is shown on the booking confirmation.

## Room service

Room service runs from 07:00 to 23:00, with a limited overnight menu available at other hours. A $5 delivery charge applies to every order.

## Bar

The lobby bar is open from 16:00 to 01:00 daily.

## Dietary requirements

The kitchen can accommodate vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free and common allergy requirements with at least 12 hours' notice. Guests with severe allergies should speak with the restaurant directly rather than relying on the buffet labels.
