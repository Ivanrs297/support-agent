# Amenities and Facilities

## Pool

The outdoor pool is open daily from 07:00 to 21:00. Towels are provided poolside. Children under 12 must be accompanied by an adult. There is no lifeguard on duty.

## Gym

The gym is on the second floor and is open 24 hours to registered guests, accessible with the room key. Guests under 16 are not permitted.

## Spa

The spa is open from 10:00 to 20:00, Tuesday through Sunday, and is closed on Mondays. Treatments must be booked in advance and cancelled at least 4 hours ahead to avoid a 50% charge.

## Wi-Fi

Wi-Fi is free throughout the property. The network is `Aurora-Guest` and the password is printed on the key card holder given at check-in. There is no data cap and no premium tier.

## Business services

A small business centre next to the lobby has two computers and a printer, available 24 hours. Printing is free up to 20 pages per stay. Two meeting rooms seating 10 and 25 can be rented; the groups desk handles those bookings.
