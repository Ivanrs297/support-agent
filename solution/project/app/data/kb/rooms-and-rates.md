# Rooms and Rates

## Room types

Hotel Aurora has 120 rooms across four categories:

- **Standard Queen** — 24 m², one queen bed, city view, sleeps 2.
- **Deluxe King** — 32 m², one king bed, partial ocean view, sleeps 2, includes a work desk and a sofa chair.
- **Family Suite** — 48 m², one king bed plus two twin beds, separate living area, sleeps 5.
- **Aurora Suite** — 65 m², one king bed, full ocean view, private terrace, sleeps 3.

All rooms include air conditioning, a safe, a mini fridge, blackout curtains, and a coffee maker.

## Nightly rates

Rates are per night, per room, and include taxes. They vary by season.

| Room type | Low season | High season |
|---|---|---|
| Standard Queen | $95 | $140 |
| Deluxe King | $135 | $190 |
| Family Suite | $210 | $290 |
| Aurora Suite | $310 | $420 |

High season runs from December 15 to January 6, and from July 1 to August 31. Every other date is low season.

## Occupancy and extra guests

Rates cover the number of guests each room sleeps. Children under 12 stay free when sharing an existing bed. An extra bed costs $25 per night and is available in Deluxe King and Family Suite rooms only. Cribs are free and must be requested at least 24 hours before arrival.
