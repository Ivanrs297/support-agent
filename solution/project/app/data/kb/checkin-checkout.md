# Check-in and Check-out

## Standard times

Check-in starts at **15:00**. Check-out is at **12:00 noon**.

Guests arriving before 15:00 can leave luggage with the front desk at no cost and use the pool and gym while they wait.

## Early check-in and late check-out

Early check-in from 12:00 is free when a room is ready, and cannot be guaranteed in advance.

Late check-out until 15:00 costs $30. Late check-out beyond 15:00 is charged as a full extra night. Both are subject to availability on the day and are requested at the front desk, not in advance.

## What is needed at check-in

A government-issued photo ID for the main guest, and the credit card used for the booking. Hotel Aurora places a $100 hold on the card as an incidental deposit, released within 5 business days of check-out.

Guests under 18 cannot check in without an accompanying adult.
