# Hotel Policies

## Pets

Hotel Aurora is pet friendly for dogs and cats up to 20 kg, with a limit of one pet per room and a fee of $35 per stay. Pets are not allowed in the restaurant, the pool area or the spa, and cannot be left alone in the room. Service animals are exempt from the fee and from the weight limit.

## Smoking

The entire property is non-smoking, indoors and on balconies. This includes vaping. A cleaning fee of $250 applies to any room where smoking is detected. There is a designated smoking area in the garden, to the left of the main entrance.

## Parking

On-site parking costs $15 per night for guests, with 60 spaces available on a first-come basis. There is no valet service. Two electric vehicle chargers are free to use.

## Accessibility

Six rooms are step-free with roll-in showers, grab bars and lowered fixtures: two Standard Queen and four Deluxe King. The lobby, restaurant, pool and gym are all step-free. The spa is reached by stairs only. Accessible rooms should be requested at booking, since they cannot be guaranteed on arrival.

## Quiet hours

Quiet hours run from 22:00 to 08:00. Events and gatherings in guest rooms are not permitted at any hour.

## Lost property

Items left behind are held for 90 days. Guests can report a lost item at the front desk or by email, and the hotel ships items back at the guest's cost.
