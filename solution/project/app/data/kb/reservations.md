# Reservations, Changes and Cancellation

## Making a reservation

Reservations can be made on the hotel website, by phone, or by email. A valid credit card is required to hold the room. The card is not charged at booking unless the rate is non-refundable.

Every confirmed reservation gets a confirmation code in the format `AUR-` followed by six digits, for example `AUR-104582`. Guests should have that code available for any change to their booking.

## Cancellation policy

Standard rates can be cancelled free of charge up to **48 hours before the check-in date**. Cancelling inside that window is charged one night at the reserved rate.

Non-refundable rates, sold at a discount, cannot be cancelled or refunded at any point. They can be changed to different dates once, subject to availability and any rate difference.

No-shows are charged the full first night and the remaining nights are released.

## Changing a reservation

Dates, room type and guest names can be changed free of charge up to 48 hours before arrival, subject to availability. If the new dates fall in a higher-priced season, the rate difference applies.

Changes are not possible once the stay has begun. A guest already in-house who wants a different room should ask the front desk, which handles it as a room change rather than a reservation change.

## Group bookings

Bookings of five rooms or more are handled by the groups desk and follow different terms: a 25% deposit at confirmation and cancellation free of charge up to 14 days before arrival. The support agent cannot modify a group booking and should hand these to a human agent.
